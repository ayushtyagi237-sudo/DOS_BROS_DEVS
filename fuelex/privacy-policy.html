Fuelex Privacy Policy

Effective October 7, 2026

Fuelex is a fuel and mileage tracker for Android. This policy explains what happens to the information you enter into the app. In short: it stays on your phone.

Information we collect

None. Fuelex has no accounts, no servers, no analytics, no advertising and no crash reporting. The app does not send any information to the developer or to third parties.

Information stored on your device

The vehicles, refills and settings you enter (for example odometer readings, fuel amounts, prices, station names and notes) are stored only in the app's private storage on your phone. Other apps cannot read them. Uninstalling Fuelex or using "Erase all data" in the app deletes them permanently.

Backups and exports

You can export a backup file or a CSV spreadsheet. This only happens when you choose to, and the file goes wherever you send it through Android's share sheet (for example your email, cloud drive or a messaging app). Those services handle the file under their own privacy policies. When you restore a backup, Fuelex reads the file you pick and nothing else.

Permissions

Fuelex uses the vibration permission for haptic feedback. Android grants all apps network access, but Fuelex does not use the internet. It does not access your location, contacts, camera, microphone or files beyond the backup file you explicitly choose.

Children

Fuelex is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.
