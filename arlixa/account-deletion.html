Account deletion

Effective 3 November 2026 · Use this page for Google Play account-deletion requests and for help if you cannot delete inside the app

You can permanently delete your Arlixa account and the personal data we hold for that account. Deletion is not the same as signing out, pausing discovery or uninstalling the app.

1. Delete in the app (fastest)

  1. Open Arlixa and sign in.
  2. Go to Settings or Privacy centre.
  3. Tap Delete account and confirm.

That starts our server-side deletion for your account on this device and clears device-only content we manage as part of that flow (for example Journal entries on that phone).

2. Request deletion by email

If you cannot open the app or reach Delete account, email us from the address on your Arlixa account:

  • To: admin.arlixa@gmail.com
  • Subject: Arlixa account deletion request
  • Body: your account email, and the words “Please permanently delete my Arlixa account and associated data.”
We only process deletion requests that we can match to an account. Using the same email as your Arlixa login helps us verify the request without collecting extra identity documents.

3. What we delete

When deletion completes, we remove (as implemented on our servers):

  • Your account credentials and session tokens;
  • Profile, preferences and privacy settings;
  • Photos you uploaded and their stored media objects;
  • Likes, passes, matches and related connection state tied to you;
  • Reveal offers and private-album grants involving you;
  • Blocks you placed, and push tokens for your devices;
  • Device-only Journal / Safe passage / Agreements cleared on the deleting device.

Conversations with other people may remain visible to them in anonymised form (“Former member”) so their history is not silently rewritten.

4. What we may keep

  • Safety reports and limited related records may be retained as needed for legal and trust-and-safety obligations, with account links removed where our deletion process provides for that;
  • Short operational server logs for a limited time;
  • Aggregated or de-identified statistics that no longer identify you.

Full detail is in our Privacy Policy (sections on retention and rights).

5. How long it takes

  • In-app deletion — processed immediately when you confirm.
  • Email requests — we aim to complete verified requests within 7 days (often sooner). We will reply when the account is gone or if we need a clarifying detail.

There is no “freeze only” option that replaces deletion for Google Play’s account deletion requirement. Once deleted, you would need a new account to use Arlixa again.
