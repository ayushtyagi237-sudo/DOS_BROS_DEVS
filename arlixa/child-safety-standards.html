Child Safety Standards

Effective 3 November 2026 · Published standards against child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE) · Adults 18+ only

This page is the publicly published child safety standards for Arlixa (the dating app operated for Google Play listing Arlixa). It states our zero-tolerance policy against child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE), including child sexual abuse material (CSAM).

1. Scope and adults-only rule

Arlixa is for consenting adults only. You must be at least 18 and provide an accurate date of birth. Anyone we believe is under 18 will have their account removed. Do not involve minors in any content, fantasy, roleplay, or conversation on Arlixa.

2. What we prohibit

Zero tolerance. Arlixa prohibits CSAE and CSAM in any form.
  • Child sexual abuse material — real, generated, or linked — including images, video, audio, and text;
  • Grooming, sexual solicitation of minors, or attempts to contact minors;
  • Any sexualisation of minors, including fictional, cartoon, or “roleplay” depictions;
  • Sharing, requesting, trading, or advertising such content on or through Arlixa.

These rules also appear in our Community Standards and Terms of Use.

3. How to report

Members can report child-safety and underage concerns in the app from profiles and conversations (confidential Report path; the reported person is not told who reported them). Categories include underage concerns and related safety issues.

You can also email the child safety contact below with subject Child safety. For urgent offline danger, contact local emergency services first.

4. What we do when we know

When we obtain actual knowledge of CSAE or CSAM on Arlixa, we take action that may include:

  • Removing the content and restricting or terminating the account;
  • Preserving evidence as required for investigation or legal process;
  • Reporting confirmed CSAM to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) CyberTipline and/or other competent regional or national authorities, as required by applicable law;
  • Cooperating with lawful requests from authorities.

5. Laws and authorities

Arlixa complies with applicable child safety laws and regulations relevant to our operation, including requirements to report confirmed CSAM to NCMEC or the appropriate regional authority where we are legally required to do so.

6. Child safety contact

Designated point of contact for CSAE / CSAM notices (including from Google Play) and questions about Arlixa’s child-safety practices:

  • Email: admin.arlixa@gmail.com
  • Subject line: Child safety

This contact is ready to speak about Arlixa’s prevention practices, enforcement, and compliance, and to take action when required.
