Privacy Policy

Effective 27 October 2026 · Applies to the Arlixa Android app and related API services

This Privacy Policy explains how Arlixa (“we”, “us”, “our”) collects, uses, stores, shares and deletes personal data when you use the Arlixa mobile application and our servers. Arlixa is built for adults exploring consensual non-monogamy. Discretion is part of the product, not a slogan.

This policy is intended to satisfy Google Play’s privacy policy and Data safety requirements, and to align with India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act) and similar expectations in other markets where we may operate. If you do not agree, do not create an account or use the app.

1. Who we are

Arlixa is operated for the Arlixa product. For privacy and child-safety enquiries write to admin.arlixa@gmail.com. The same entity name that appears on our Google Play listing is the controller of personal data described here.

2. Scope

This policy covers:

  • The Arlixa Android application distributed through Google Play;
  • Our API and media services that the app talks to over HTTPS;
  • Operator tooling used only to moderate photos, review reports and keep the service safe.

It does not cover third-party websites you open from links inside messages or profiles.

3. What we collect

We collect only what is needed to run Arlixa as a dating and connection product. We do not currently use advertising SDKs, third-party analytics SDKs or crash reporters in the app. If that changes, we will update this policy and the Play Data safety form before shipping.

Account and security

  • Email address;
  • Password (stored only as a one-way hash; we cannot read your password);
  • Email verification and password-reset codes (short-lived);
  • Session tokens (access and refresh tokens) used to keep you signed in securely.

Profile

  • Display name, optional pronouns;
  • Date of birth (used to verify you are 18+ and to compute age; exact DOB is not shown to other members);
  • Gender and optional self-description;
  • City and optional neighbourhood-level place for introductions;
  • Bio, prompts, interests, intentions and relationship structure you choose to provide;
  • Capacity (how much room you have for new connections);
  • Discovery preferences and privacy settings (including notification preview mode).

Photos

  • Images you upload for a public profile cover or a private album;
  • Moderation status (pending, approved, rejected);
  • Technical processing: uploads are re-encoded; EXIF/GPS metadata is stripped before storage.

Social graph and safety

  • Discovery decisions (pass, interested, saved);
  • Likes, matches and connection membership (including mute and read state);
  • Messages you send in connections;
  • Step-by-step reveal offers (area, photos, contact) between two connected members;
  • Private-album access requests, grants, declines and revocations;
  • Blocks;
  • Reports you submit (category, optional details) and our handling status;
  • In-app Activity / notification history rows (type of event, related members, read state — not full message bodies).

Device and notifications

  • Expo push tokens and platform (e.g. Android) when you allow notifications, so we can deliver alerts through Expo and Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM);
  • Basic request metadata on our servers (time, route, status, duration, request id) for reliability — not message bodies or passwords.

What we do not collect

  • Precise GPS coordinates from the device;
  • Contacts, call logs or SMS;
  • Microphone or camera capture (you pick photos from your library; camera permission is not requested);
  • Payment card details (membership is coming soon; the app takes no payment today);
  • Advertising identifiers for ad tracking.

4. What stays only on your device

The following never leave your phone to our servers. They are stored locally and are erased when you delete your account from the device or clear app data:

  • Journal — private notes to yourself;
  • Safe passage — meeting plans and trusted contacts you record for first meetings;
  • Our agreements — relationship agreements you keep for review with a partner on one device.

If you copy text from these into a profile field or a message, that copy becomes ordinary account or message data and is handled under the rest of this policy.

5. How we use data

  • Create and secure your account;
  • Show you Discover introductions and show your profile to others according to your settings;
  • Enable likes, matches, messaging, mute, closing kindly and unmatching;
  • Enforce private albums and step-by-step reveal only between people who opt in;
  • Moderate photos before they are visible to others;
  • Review reports and keep people safe;
  • Send optional push and in-app Activity alerts (message, like, match, close, album request/grant), respecting mute and your notification preview mode;
  • Provide export and account deletion;
  • Operate, debug and protect the service (abuse, fraud, outages).

We do not sell personal data. We do not use your Journal or message contents for advertising.

6. How we share data

Other members

Other members see only what your visibility and connection state allow — for example public profile fields, approved public photos, messages in a shared conversation, and mutual reveal values once unlocked. They never receive your email, password, exact date of birth, exact coordinates, Journal, Safe passage or Agreements.

Service providers (processors)

We use infrastructure providers under contract to host and deliver Arlixa:

  • Cloud hosting and object storage for the API and private media;
  • Managed database hosting;
  • Email delivery for verification and password reset;
  • Expo and Google Firebase Cloud Messaging to deliver push notifications when you opt in (they process device push tokens and notification payloads we send).

These providers process data on our instructions to run Arlixa. They are not sold your data for their own marketing.

Legal and safety

We may disclose information if required by law, to respond to lawful requests, or to protect someone’s vital interests (for example imminent harm or child safety reports), as narrowly as reasonably possible.

7. What other members can see

  • Public profile — fields and approved public photos you make discoverable, subject to capacity and discovery filters;
  • Private photos — only after you grant access (or after mutual photo reveal unlocks access). A request alone never reveals them;
  • Distance — only coarse bands (for example “Within 15 km”) when you allow distance to be shown; never a pin on a map;
  • Notification previews — you choose full, name only, or discreet. Discreet alerts contain no names or message text.

8. Location

Arlixa does not request continuous device GPS for tracking. You select a city or neighbourhood from our place list. Distance shown to others is approximated into bands. Exact coordinates are not accepted from the client for discovery and are never shown to other members.

9. Photos and moderation

Uploaded photos are stored privately. In production, photos remain pending until approved by our moderation process. Pending photos are visible to you as the owner but not to other members. Media is served through short-lived, permission-checked links — not public “anyone with the link” URLs.

10. Messages and reveals

Messages exist so two matched people can talk. We store them to deliver the product (including when you reopen a conversation). Closing kindly keeps a readable, read-only thread for both people; unmatching removes the conversation for both. Reveal values are returned only to the two participants once both have offered that step and earlier steps are unlocked.

In-app Activity history stores structured event types (for example “message” or “like”), not a second copy of full message bodies.

11. Notifications and push tokens

If you allow notifications, we store a push token for your device. You can revoke OS permission at any time; we also remove tokens when they are invalid or when you sign out / delete your account. Muting a connection suppresses message pushes and message Activity rows for that connection.

12. Security

  • HTTPS for API and media traffic;
  • Hashed passwords; short-lived access tokens and rotating refresh tokens;
  • Private object storage for photos;
  • Operator access to moderation and reports is restricted and audited.

No method of transmission or storage is perfectly secure. Please use a unique password and protect your device.

13. Retention

  • Account and profile data — while your account is active;
  • Messages and connections — while the conversation exists under our product rules;
  • Activity notifications — pruned (for example older than 90 days or beyond a per-user cap);
  • Reports — may be retained as needed for safety review even after an account is deleted, with account links removed where our deletion process provides for that;
  • Server access logs — short operational windows;
  • Device-only Journal / Safe passage / Agreements — until you clear them or uninstall.

14. Your rights, export and deletion

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, export or erase personal data.

  • In the app — Privacy centre and Settings let you update privacy preferences, export a copy of data we hold (plus device-only sections from this device), and delete your account.
  • Account deletion — removes your account, profile, photos (and stored objects), decisions, tokens and related member data as implemented on our servers. Conversations with others may remain visible to them in anonymised form (“Former member”) so their history is not silently rewritten. Device-only content is cleared on that device as part of deletion.
  • Email us — admin.arlixa@gmail.com if you cannot use in-app controls.
Google Play. Account deletion permanently deletes associated account data under our deletion process; we do not treat “freeze” as a substitute for deletion.

15. Children and age

Arlixa is strictly for adults 18 years or older. We do not knowingly allow minors to create accounts. We require an accurate date of birth. If we learn someone under 18 has an account, we will terminate it. See also our Community Standards on child safety.

16. International processing

Our servers and providers may process data in countries other than your own (including where our cloud region and database are hosted). We take steps appropriate to the sensitivity of dating data when engaging processors.

17. Legal bases and India (DPDP)

Where the DPDP Act applies, we process personal data for the lawful purposes of providing the service you request, securing the service, and complying with law. Consent toggles in the product (for example analytics or marketing) are stored; we do not currently run analytics or marketing programmes against them. If we start doing so, we will only do it consistent with those choices and this policy.

18. Changes

We may update this policy. The “Effective” date at the top will change. Material changes will be reflected here and, where appropriate, noticed in the app. Continued use after the effective date means you accept the updated policy.
