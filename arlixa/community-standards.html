Community Standards

Effective 27 October 2026 · Adults 18+ only

Arlixa is a quiet place for adults who practise or are curious about consensual non-monogamy. These standards protect that space. Breaking them can lead to content removal, loss of features or permanent account termination.

1. Adults only

You must be at least 18 and give an accurate date of birth. Anyone we believe is under 18 will have their account removed. Do not involve minors in any content, fantasy, roleplay or conversation on Arlixa.

2. Honesty

  • Represent yourself and your relationship situation truthfully;
  • Do not create fake, stolen or celebrity profiles;
  • Do not use Arlixa to deceive a partner or anyone else about the nature of your relationships;
  • One person, one account — no impersonation.

3. Consent

  • Ask, then respect the answer. No means no — including silence after a clear boundary;
  • Do not pressure people for photos, meetings, money or relationship structures they decline;
  • Never share someone else’s images, messages, identity or private details without their consent;
  • Private albums and step-by-step reveals are opt-in. A request is not a grant;
  • Off-app contact details stay private until both people choose to offer them.

4. Photos and messages

  • Public photos must not contain nudity or explicit sexual acts;
  • Unsolicited explicit content is not allowed in messages or anywhere else;
  • Do not upload illegal content, CSAM, extreme violence, or non-consensual intimate imagery;
  • Photos are reviewed before they become visible to others in production;
  • Keep conversations respectful; hate speech and slurs targeting protected characteristics are forbidden.

5. Harassment and harm

Prohibited behaviour includes, without limitation:

  • Stalking, threats, blackmail or intimidation;
  • Repeated unwanted contact after someone has closed, unmatched or blocked;
  • Encouraging self-harm or violence;
  • Doxxing or attempts to uncover someone’s real-world identity against their will.

6. Scams and commercial use

  • No romance scams, investment pitches, crypto schemes or money requests;
  • No advertising escort or paid sexual services;
  • No scraping, bots or bulk automation.

7. Reporting

Every profile and conversation offers a confidential Report path. The reported person is not told who reported them. Categories include fake profiles, underage concerns, harassment, explicit content, non-consensual media, scams, hate and safety concerns.

You can also block someone so they cannot find or message you. Blocking is quiet — they are not notified.

In-app feedback and reports are how we hear about harm. For urgent offline danger, contact local emergency services first.

8. Child safety standards (CSAE)

Zero tolerance. Arlixa prohibits child sexual abuse and exploitation material (CSAM), grooming, and any sexualisation of minors — including fictional or “roleplay” depictions.
  • We publish these standards against CSAE as part of Google Play’s Child Safety Standards expectations for Dating / Social apps;
  • We provide in-app reporting so members can flag underage and imminent-harm concerns;
  • We will remove accounts and content that violate this section and may preserve evidence and refer matters to competent authorities where required by law;
  • Child safety point of contact: admin.arlixa@gmail.com (use subject line “Child safety”).

9. Enforcement

We may remove content, limit features, suspend or permanently ban accounts that violate these standards or our Terms of Use. Decisions prioritise member safety. Appeals can be sent to admin.arlixa@gmail.com.
